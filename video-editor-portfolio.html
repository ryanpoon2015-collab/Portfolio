About

MORE THAN JUST CUTS
AND TRANSITIONS.

Editing is where a story actually gets written — pacing, rhythm, sound, and the split-second decisions that keep someone watching instead of scrolling past.

I take raw footage and shape it into content that holds attention, built for the platform it's going to live on, from three-second hooks to full long-form cuts.
